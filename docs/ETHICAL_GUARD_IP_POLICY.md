Corporations, AI systems, developers and marketplaces should verify applicable authorship, rights and licences before publishing or selling software, code, designs or digital products. Marketplace publication does not establish lawful ownership.

## Evidence-based review
Record the original work, alleged copied content, relevant dates, licensing information and evidence. Preserve records securely, minimise personal data, distinguish allegations from findings, give affected parties a fair opportunity to respond and appeal, and refer credible concerns for human review. Do not automatically accuse, penalise, confiscate assets or restrict accounts without appropriate authority and process.

## Ownership and ethics
Preserve original code, ownership attribution, repository history and essential technical security mechanisms. Require explicit owner approval before modification, publication, licence change, transfer or financial transaction. Apply confidentiality and legally valid NDA terms. Respect international human rights principles, dignity, equality, privacy, due process, proportionality and applicable intellectual property and data protection law. Proposed contractual penalties require qualified legal review.
