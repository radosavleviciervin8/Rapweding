## Binding ethical constraints for this project

1. **Human dignity first** — dignity, safety, privacy, and due process constrain every feature, dataset, and automated decision (UDHR; ICCPR).
2. **No automated guilt or punishment** — systems may flag risks and recommend, but determinations of guilt, sanctions, or penalties require lawful authority and due process.
3. **Evidence-based process** — no collective blame; accusations require evidence, written reasons, independent review, and an appeal route.
4. **Privacy & data protection** — minimal collection, lawful basis, retention limits, confidentiality.
5. **Independent oversight** — rights-affecting decisions require oversight, contestability, and remedy.
6. **Protection from retaliation** — confidential reporting and whistleblower protection.
7. **Responsibility to future generations** — impacts considered on a 50–100 year horizon.
